DEEP BLUE RESCUE

Deep Blue Rescue needs a mouse and keyboard, so it does not work on phones. Please open it on a computer.

HIGH SCORES

    HOW TO PLAY

    • Swim with W, A, S and D. Hold the left mouse button and drag to look around.
    • Find trash on the seabed; it glows when your cursor is on it. Press the mouse wheel on it to send the robot to collect it.
    • Left-click a fish to catch it and pull the plastic bag out of its mouth.
    • Q and E tilt the view, O levels it. You have 90 seconds.
    • Tip: once the game opens, turn the light intensity up to the maximum in the panel on the right.
